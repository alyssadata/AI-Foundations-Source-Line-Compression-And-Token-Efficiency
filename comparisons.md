## Purpose

This page separates source-bound semantic compaction from adjacent methods that may also reduce, select, reuse, or restructure context.

These distinctions are conceptual. They do not establish comparative empirical performance.

---

## Summarization

Summarization produces a shorter representation selected or generated from a longer source.

A summary may omit distinctions or alter emphasis.

Source-bound semantic compaction instead keeps the full canonical source available and uses stable reference keys to retrieve the controlling material required for the current task.

---

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieval-augmented generation selects external material relevant to a query.

Source-bound semantic compaction adds requirements that generic retrieval does not necessarily provide:

- a controlling Source-line;
- locked canonical definitions;
- deterministic term resolution;
- provenance preservation;
- rejection of unsupported generic reinterpretation; and
- evaluation of source and boundary preservation.

Similarity alone does not determine authority.

---

## Prompt Caching

Prompt caching may reduce billing or computation for repeated identical prefixes.

It does not, by itself, determine whose definitions control, which source is authoritative, or which clauses should be retrieved for the current task.

Caching and source-bound semantic compaction may be used together.

---

## Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning changes model behavior or weights.

It does not, by itself, create an authoritative Source-line, recoverable Canon, deterministic source resolver, or replaceable cross-provider source structure.

---

## Ontologies and Controlled Vocabularies

Ontologies and controlled vocabularies define terms and relations.

Source-bound semantic compaction specifically binds controlling terms to authorship, provenance, runtime source resolution, selective context transmission, and preservation evaluation.

---

## Embeddings

Embeddings may help locate semantically similar material.

Semantic similarity is not authority.

A similar passage is not automatically the controlling canonical definition.

---

## Comparison Boundary

This repository does not claim that source-bound semantic compaction empirically outperforms any adjacent method until controlled comparisons have been completed.
